## Verification and assessment

Independent run: Python suite **35/35 passed**; `make gsw-selfcheck` passed with
GSW 12/12, metrics 9/9, license tests 14/14, license checker, and ping-frame
self-check.

The parser bounds retained input to one configured maximum packet and correctly
handles the tested split/coalesced fragments, CRC/version checks, and
resynchronization after invalid bytes. The ping reader clamps serial reads to
the remaining monotonic deadline and 0.1 s polling limit, rejects data returned
after the deadline, caps captured telemetry at 64 packets, and waits for the
exact S17.2 echo tuple rather than any service-17 packet.

## Findings and limits

- A plausible CCSDS header with an incomplete but permitted declared length can
  hold a later valid packet until the candidate completes or the overall
  deadline expires. The implementation deliberately does not guess an inner
  packet boundary; this is bounded head-of-line/false-timeout risk, not
  unconditional resynchronization.
- The fixed echo tuple does not prove response freshness or uniquely correlate
  the response to the just-sent request; sequence/source identity is not used.
  No controlled profile/requirement currently authorizes a stronger claim.
- Tests use synthetic input. They do not establish independent-ground-segment
  interoperability, actual serial/target behavior, timing on target, or full T7
  qualification.

## Disposition

Accept the T7 code/test delta for the bounded host behaviors above. Keep
NC-EXT-03 OPEN for independent ground-segment end-to-end evidence and any
approved response-correlation criterion. T7 remains **not fully qualified**.
