## Statement on rev 1.4

**Rev 1.4 is accurate on the substantive confirmation scope**, with reservations on
internal consistency and on the evidence-binding layer: (A) the NC-1 oracle is
discriminating in the correct direction, the healthy control is stated, HS-P1 is
correctly the non-discriminating negative case — the residual exposure is in the
*unpinned inputs* and the *undeclared mock capability*, not in the oracle's
logic; (B) HS-P5 separates stimulus from oracle and NC-3 discriminates in logic,
but the oracle's strict equality can fail a conforming binding; (C) the probe
attribution, the measured/not-established split and the §5.1 placement are
probe-accurate and no model claim exceeds the retained probe; (D) every
F-18..F-24 claim traces to a verbatim quote and **no clock, timeout, instance or
reset policy is adopted anywhere** — but the companion proposal at the same
revision still selects the superseded instance; (E) the case sets are consistent
with three residual gaps; (F) **status honesty is retained and accurate**, but
the identity layer is not — the manifest does not verify and the review-coverage
attribution overstates coverage.
