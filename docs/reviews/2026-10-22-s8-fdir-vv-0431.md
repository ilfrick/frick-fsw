## (c) Arithmetic (editorial only)

Retrieval log:126 reads "16 777 200 (0xFFF3D8)". **16 777 200 = 0xFFFFF0;
`0xFFF3D8` = 16 774 104 (−3096)** — the hex annotation is wrong. **No conclusion
changes**: the counter clock is 1.6024 MHz versus 1.6021 MHz and the reaction
window 2.5552 ms versus 2.5560 ms, so "≈1.6 MHz", "≈410 MHz" and "≈2.6 ms"
stand; it is a labelled hypothetical inference, not a chosen value. **The spec
body uses the correct form**: decimal 16 777 200 (spec:244–250) and `0xFFFFF0`
in F-16 (spec:365); `0xFFF3D8` appears nowhere in the spec.
