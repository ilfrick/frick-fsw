**Graded conclusion**

- **PA (N20): APPROVED WITH COMMENTS (0 Major, 7 Minor, 5 Comments).**

The M16 phase products are, in substance, coherent and correctly scoped: the new
requirements `SSS-REQ-027`/`SRS-REQ-054` are uniquely identified and bidirectionally
linked (machine-verified `trace.py` PASS `96/55/27/14/51`), M16 is consistently
recorded as **planned** across every status source, the M12 "integration" reframe
is stated in the roadmap/README/gap-analysis, the metrics panel matches
`metrics.py`, the `CHANGELOG` records the change with its origin, and no record
claims M16 is delivered. No Major finding exists and none of the findings defeats
a requirement or a safety barrier.

However, the M16 change does **not** qualify for a clean APPROVED. Seven Minor
non-conformances remain open, all of the same class (phase products **modified or
superseded without the accompanying currency/history obligation**): the
human-readable `traceability.md` was not regenerated, and the document-level
revision History was not updated for `development-plan.md`,
`component-gap-analysis.md` and `handover.md` (which also omits M16 from its
milestone list); `README.md` still carries pre-M16 counts inconsistent with its
own panel; and the N19 loop-audit reports counts/closure that do not match its
stated baseline and the still-open M16 reviews. Record truthfulness (finding 7)
is the PA-critical item: the review semaphores are GREEN at HEAD while the
M16 N14/N17/N20 reviews are open and untracked in the status records.

Process obligations: **N15** applicable gates pass; **N14** (R3, FRICK-REVIEW-0064)
and **N17** (R4, FRICK-REVIEW-0065) have been run independently and both return
APPROVED WITH COMMENTS with open Minor NCs; **N20** is this review. All Minor
findings from all three reviews must be corrected (or justified) and re-verified,
and the reviews repeated to closure (norm N12) before the M16 loop is closed.
The M16 change therefore **passes the N20 PA review only as APPROVED WITH COMMENTS**,
conditional on the tracked Minor NCs being closed; a clean "APPROVED" is **not**
granted.
