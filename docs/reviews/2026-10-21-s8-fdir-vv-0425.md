## Reviewer disposition

**Qualified yes as a decision agenda; no as an approvable implementation
design** — the structure, the reference inventory and the separation are
sound, and the content corrections of this revision address the four blocking
classes the reviewer identified. The recommended D-01 disposition is option
(b): approve structure and inventory, and return the content decisions for a
bounded correction cycle (now applied) before any register access.

**Residual owner questions** are carried in the spec as M-1..M-16 and
D-01..D-15; the reviewer's own list additionally asks about the reaction-window
sufficiency, the 250 ms relationship, the reset scope, the design-time state,
the public-source-only basis, the gating ownership, the N18 gate meaning, the
HIL thresholds, the feed cadence in the interim, the escalation record and the
review scheduling.

**Confidence:** high for the reproduced hashes, the claim checks, the status
honesty and the decision-table structure; medium for the emulator-model
behaviour (the reviewer's environment did not hold the pinned image, so the
model findings are stated against upstream `master` and are corroborated by the
embedded-safety reviewer's measurements against the pinned image).
