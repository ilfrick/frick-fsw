## 1. Scope

Reviewed the fifth M11 HAL-coverage increment: **NET exercised in the Renode
functional run** by a socket round trip through the pluggable `FF_NetStack_t`
contract using the reference loopback stack (`FF_NetStackLoopback`), with the
result folded into the `RENODE_TEST: PASS` marker (`hal_net=PASS`).

Verified (with reproduction, not by reading prose):

- the Renode image cross-build (`frick-rtems-toolchain:local`,
  `-DFF_PLATFORM=rtems -DFF_ENABLE_RENODE_TEST=ON`) and `make renode`
  (`renode_vv.sh`) against the fully re-built ELF;
- the host gates: `ctest` (14/14) and the CTest `netloop` case, the traceability
  gate `trace.py`, and the `clang-format` gate in `frick-vv:local`;
- the traceability of SRS-REQ-039 (implementation paths, `tests`, `lean_note`)
  and the **strength/qualification** of the newly added `ci:renode` evidence;
- whether `renode_hal_net_check` is a genuine contract exercise or a
  self-referential check (read `tests/unit/test_netloop.c` and the app check side
  by side);
- the milestone completion claim against OBJ-19/M11 as declared in
  `docs/development-plan.md`.

Excluded: on-target lwIP/GEM netif+PHY behaviour (GAP-A5); real-silicon timing;
documentation revision/history currency (FRICK-REVIEW-0048).

Assumption recorded: the reviewed artefact is the *uncommitted working tree* on
base `205bfc5`; no committed revision of the NET delta exists yet.

- The recorded claim reproduces end-to-end: the loopback round trip passes on
  the emulated PolarFire SoC and is folded, by a verified conjunction, into the
  `RENODE_TEST: PASS` marker. The `verification-results.md` row and the

## 4. Conclusion

**R2 (SW V&V): APPROVED WITH COMMENTS.** No Major finding. The core V&V claim
reproduces: the RTEMS/RISC-V image cross-builds, `make renode` yields
`RENODE_TEST: PASS` with `hal_net=PASS`, the host gates are green
(14/14, `trace.py` PASS 94/54/26/14/51, format clean), and the marker
faithfully gates the NET check through a verified boolean conjunction. The
`renode_hal_net_check` function **does genuinely exercise the
`FF_NetStack_t` contract** end-to-end through its vtable on the target — it is
**not** a self-referential "marker checks itself" check. However, (i) it is
implementation-coupled (peer = the same loopback implementation), a strict
subset of the qualified host unit test, and touched no emulated peripheral;
(ii) the `ci:renode` evidence added for SRS-REQ-039 is not bidirectionally
traced to the requirement in the CI marker, is not mapped to a qualified tool,
and rests on testware that is not registered; (iii) the "NET class done"
milestone claim does not meet OBJ-19/M11's declared criterion. These are
Minor/Comments to be corrected or justified and tracked to closure (norm N12)
before the increment is considered closed:

- **R2-01** milestone claim vs OBJ-19/M11 (peripheral-bound, lwIP) — and the
  standing conflict with R4-02;
- **R2-02** `ci:renode` not bound to SRS-REQ-039 in the workflow evidence marker;
- **R2-03** `ci:renode` not traced to a qualified tool; Renode testware
  unregistered; N10 gate not implemented as documented;
- **R2-04** oracle independence limited; on-target coverage is a subset of
  `test_netloop.c`.

Comments only (no derogation): R2-05 (gate integrity — positive), R2-06
(recurrence of the silent `docker cp` false FAIL, R1-08), R2-07 ("end-to-end"
wording).
