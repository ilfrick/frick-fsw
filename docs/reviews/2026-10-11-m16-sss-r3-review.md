**Graded conclusion**

- **R3 (Systems Engineering, N14): APPROVED WITH COMMENTS.** `SSS-REQ-027` and
  `SRS-REQ-054` are uniquely identified, correctly linked in both directions
  (machine-verified: `trace.py` PASS 96/55/27/14/51), attributed to a defined
  milestone (M16) and consistent with the mission context; no Major finding
  exists and none of the findings defeats the requirement or a safety barrier.
  The new requirements are, however, not yet fully singular, bounded or
  objectively testable (findings 1-3), and the M12/M16 split is only partially
  propagated (findings 4-7): the development plan and gap analysis still frame
  M12 itself as the in-house-engine fallback, the human traceability matrix is
  stale, and the M16 execution order conflicts with the PUS S18 surface that
  M15 (scheduled later) owns. These Minor findings must be corrected or justified
  and re-verified (norm N12) before the M16 loop is closed.

Overall: **APPROVED WITH COMMENTS** (0 Major, 7 Minor, 3 Comments).
