Each entry maintains its own absolute next-release target; the mixed-period regression checks 30/6/3 dispatch counts for 1/5/10 s entries over 30 s, and missed slots are advanced without a catch-up burst. This is bounded host evidence, not target schedulability or mission suitability.

## Open finding

The supported time range and exhaustion behavior are unspecified and untested. Track as NC-EXT-90 and obtain an approved time-domain/response policy; do not silently invent mission behavior.

The no-burst late-slot policy is provisional and requires mission-authority approval. Poll cadence coverage does not exhaust all phase relationships. This is not a schedulability, target, safety, or release approval.
